# San José City Council — June 9, 2026

## Overview

The Council took up three ceremonial proclamations, a consent calendar with five items, and one construction contract action. The largest dollar figure was an $11,678,000 construction contract awarded for security upgrades at the regional wastewater facility (Item 6.1). All motions at this meeting passed unanimously; there were no split votes.

## Ceremonial Items

Councilmember George Casey presented a proclamation marking the 250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence (Item 1.1). Councilmember Anthony Tordillos presented a proclamation for Philippines Independence Day (Item 1.2). Councilmember Peter Ortiz presented a proclamation for Portuguese Heritage Month (Item 1.3).

## Consent Calendar

The Council approved its consent calendar as a whole on a motion by Councilmember Bien Doan, seconded by Rosemary Kamei, by a vote of 11-0-0 (Item 2.8). This action included final adoption of Ordinance No. 31328, establishing the East Village Business Improvement District under the Parking and Business Improvement Area Law of 1989 (Item [no number]).

The consent calendar also included a Third Amendment to the Standard Consultant Agreement with CSG Advisors Incorporated, increasing the contract by $250,000 and raising its total value to $1,000,000 (Item 2.8).

The Council approved free use of the Rotunda, Wing Meeting Rooms, Council Chambers, East Plaza, West Plaza, and South Plaza for the 4th Annual Neighborhoods Conference on October 3, 2026, hosted by the Department of Parks, Recreation and Neighborhood Services, adopting Resolution No. RES2026-176 (Item 2.9).

The Council retroactively approved free use of the Janet Gray Hayes Rotunda for the African American Community Service Agency's Juneteenth Power 50 Freedom Ball, sponsored by the Office of Mayor Mahan as a City Council-sponsored special event to expend city funds and accept donations of materials and services (Item 2.13).

## Other Actions

On a motion by Councilmember Domingo Candelas, seconded by Councilmember Anthony Tordillos, the Council voted 11-0-0 to accept a bid report and award a construction contract for the 9885 Camera and Card Reader Upgrades Project to Blocka Construction Inc. in the amount of $11,678,000 (Item 6.1). The Council also approved a 10% contingency of $1,167,800 and adopted 2025-2026 Appropriation Ordinance No. 31331 amending the San José-Santa Clara Treatment Plant Capital Fund (Item 6.1).

## Notable Votes and Financial Actions

- The Council approved a $250,000 increase to the CSG Advisors Incorporated consulting agreement, raising the contract total to $1,000,000 (Item 2.8).
- The Council awarded an $11,678,000 construction contract to Blocka Construction Inc. for the 9885 Camera and Card Reader Upgrades Project (Item 6.1).
- The Council approved a 10% contingency of $1,167,800 for the same project (Item 6.1).
